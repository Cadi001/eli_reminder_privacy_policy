# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 12, 2025

## 1. Introduction

Eli Reminder ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Reminder Data

All reminder data, including reminder titles, schedules, times, and notification preferences, is stored locally on your device using secure local storage (Hive database). This data is never transmitted to our servers or third-party services, except as necessary for notification delivery through your device's operating system.

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information

- **Device Information**: We may collect basic device information (device model, operating system version) for app functionality and compatibility purposes.
- **Usage Data**: We do not actively collect usage analytics or tracking data. However, your device's operating system may collect certain information automatically.
- **Advertising Data**: When you view advertisements through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), Google may collect information according to their privacy policy. See Section 4 for more details.

### 2.3 App Preferences

App preferences such as theme settings, notification permissions, and quiet hour preferences are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to external servers.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve the App's functionality
- Deliver reminders and notifications to your device
- Ensure app compatibility with your device
- Display advertisements through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)
- Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties.

## 4. Third-Party Services

### 4.1 Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google may collect and process information about you and your device, including:

- Device identifiers (such as advertising IDs)
- IP address
- Device information (model, operating system)
- App usage data
- Location data (if permitted by your device settings)

Google uses this information to provide personalized advertisements. For more information about how Google uses your data, please review Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and Google's AdMob Privacy Policy at https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device's advertising settings:

- **Android**: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization
- **iOS**: Settings > Privacy > Apple Advertising > Limit Ad Tracking

### 4.2 Notification Services

The App uses your device's notification services (Awesome Notifications) to deliver reminders. These services operate according to your device's operating system settings and privacy policies.

### 4.3 Home Widget Service

The App may use home widget services to display reminder information on your device's home screen. Widget data is stored locally on your device.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

### 5.1 Local Storage

All reminder data and app preferences are stored locally on your device using secure local storage (Hive database). We do not maintain servers or cloud storage for your reminder data.

### 5.2 Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your data stored locally on your device. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

### 5.3 Data Retention

Your reminder data is retained on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. When you uninstall the App, all locally stored data is deleted from your device.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

### 6.1 Access and Control

You have the right to:

- Access your reminder data at any time through the App
- Modify or delete your reminder data at any time
- Delete all data by uninstalling the App
- Request information about data collection practices

### 6.2 California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

- **Right to Know**: You have the right to request information about the categories and specific pieces of personal information we collect, use, and disclose.
- **Right to Delete**: You have the right to request deletion of your personal information.
- **Right to Opt-Out**: You have the right to opt-out of the sale of personal information. However, we do not sell your personal information.
- **Right to Non-Discrimination**: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided in Section 11.

### 6.3 Other State Privacy Rights

Residents of other states may have additional privacy rights under state-specific laws. Please contact us for more information about your rights.

### 6.4 Advertising Preferences

You can control personalized advertising through your device settings as described in Section 4.1.

## 7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13 ("Children"). We do not knowingly collect personal information from Children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your Child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from Children without verification of parental consent, we will take steps to remove that information from our systems.

If you are between the ages of 13 and 18 (or the age of majority in your jurisdiction), you represent that you have your parent's or guardian's permission to use the App.

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties. We may disclose your information only in the following circumstances:

- **Service Providers**: We may share information with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf, such as Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for advertising services. These service providers are bound by contractual obligations to keep your information confidential.
- **Legal Requirements**: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court or a government agency).
- **Business Transfers**: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.
- **With Your Consent**: We may disclose your information with your explicit consent.

## 9. International Data Transfers

Your data is stored locally on your device and is not transferred internationally. However, third-party services (such as Google Mobile Ads) may process your information in countries outside your jurisdiction. By using the App, you consent to the transfer of information to these third-party services in accordance with their privacy policies.
